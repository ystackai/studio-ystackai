## How to Play
1. Click **START SHIFT** to begin
2. Use **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** to move your agent around the factory
3. Walk to a station with a fire to **extinguish it** (or press Space/X)
4. Build stations produce builds (green ◈) that auto-propagate to Test → Ship
5. Ship builds for bonus points — don't let fires destroy the factory!
6. Survive 3 minutes across escalating waves
