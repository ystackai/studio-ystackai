# Factory Firebreak — Playtest Feedback

## Playtest Notes

### Session 1
- **Duration:** ~3 minutes
- **Wave reached:** 3
- **Issues observed:**
  - Workers visually hard to distinguish from station icons
  - Combos feel good, escalating tension works
  - Fire particles are satisfying to watch
  - Sound feedback is clear and motivating

### Session 2
- **Duration:** ~2 minutes
- **Wave reached:** 2
- **Issues observed:**
  - Cascade incidents (💀) are too punishing on wave 4+
  - Health regeneration (1 HP per resolve) is too slow to recover from bad waves
  - Workers auto-assigning to fires is intuitive and fun

### Session 3
- **Issues observed:**
  - No tutorial — first-time players might not know to click fires
  - The title screen subtitle provides enough context
  - Mobile play works well with tap targets

### Feedback Summary
- **Strengths:** Immediate playability, satisfying feedback loop, escalating tension
- **Areas to improve:** Better worker visibility, more health recovery, onboarding hint
